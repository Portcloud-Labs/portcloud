Services, not servers

买你需要的能力，
不是一台等待配置的机器。Get the capability you need, not a machine waiting to be configured.

从你的实际需求出发，Portcloud 把能力变成可以直接使用的服务，而不是让你先买一台主机，再自己把它变成想要的东西。Your needs should come first. Portcloud turns them into ready-to-use services, instead of making you buy a machine and build everything yourself.

01

选择能力，不选择机器配置Choose capabilities, not machine specs

先看服务能帮你解决什么问题，而不是从 CPU、内存和容器开始。Start with the problem a service solves, not CPU, memory or containers.

02

接入使用，不从零部署Connect and use, not deploy from scratch

进入服务网站，按服务的接入方式配置使用，无需先部署你自己的服务实例。Visit the service website and follow its setup instructions. No need to deploy your own instance first.

03

专注你的应用和工作流Focus on your apps and workflows

基础设施由 Portcloud 管理，你把精力留给真正想完成的事情。Portcloud manages the infrastructure so you can focus on what you want to accomplish.

Configure → Use

配置即用，还是从零搭建。Configure and use, or build from scratch.

三步开始使用，还是六步搭建维护。Three steps to start using — or six steps to build and maintain.
